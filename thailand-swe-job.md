## The visa reality that filters your list

- **BOI-promoted companies are the cheat code** — exempt from the Thai:foreigner ratio, work permit via the One Stop Service Centre. For an **IT specialist** they need you earning **≥THB 50,000/month with a relevant bachelor's** (≥75,000 without). Most Tier 1 names above fit this.
- **Non-BOI Thai companies**: you generally need **4 full-time SSO-registered Thai employees per foreigner** (~THB 2M paid-up capital per permit) — this is enforced at the annual visa extension, so small local startups often *can't* legally hire you. Hence all the "Thai nationals only" ads.
- **Working remotely for a foreign employer**: the **DTV** gives 5 years, 180 days per entry (extendable +180), needs **THB 500,000** seasoned ~3 months. It does *not* permit working for Thai companies.
- **High earners**: LTR visa (~US$80k/yr income) includes work permit rights.
